Clinical Disclaimer

This application is a clinical decision-support and research-recording tool only. It does not replace clinical judgment, examination, formal radiological reporting, or the standard of care.

  • Classifications, scores, and measurements are aids. Verify every result against the original source criteria and the clinical picture.
  • Radiographic measurements depend on image quality, calibration, and point placement by the user.
  • The developers accept no liability for clinical decisions made using this application.
  • Patient data are stored locally and unencrypted on this device. You are responsible for compliance with your institution's privacy and ethics requirements.
